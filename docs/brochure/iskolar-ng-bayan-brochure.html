Responsible by design

The AI advises. The office decides.

Screening support is only useful if staff can trust it and check it. These safeguards are built into every step.

  • Advisory results onlyThe system never awards or rejects a scholarship on its own. Applicants who fail a rule are flagged, not rejected, and every result goes to staff.
  • Explanations that add upEach result shows how much every factor raised or lowered the applicant's chance. The amounts add up exactly to the percentage shown.
  • No repeat screening without causeEach result stores a fingerprint of its inputs and the model version. An application that has not changed is not screened again.
  • Archived, never deletedPast applicants and their outcomes stay on record and searchable for later scholarship cycles.
  • Staff sign-in and activity logOnly signed-in staff can work in the system, and each action is recorded with who did it and when.
  • Privacy from the startThe model was trained on synthetic records. No real applicant data is kept in the source code or its backups.

Final decisions on every scholarship rest with the Local Youth Development Office.

AI-assisted screening

Rules first, then nine factors

Office policy is checked directly. Only applicants who pass every rule are scored by the AI, a Random Forest of 300 decision trees.

Step 1 · Eligibility rules

  • Entrance exam score of 80% or higher
  • Academic average above 75
  • Not a 4Ps or SPES beneficiary
  • No other scholar in the family

Fail any rule and the AI gives no score. The application is flagged for staff to confirm.

How the ranking is used

  • Ranked only against the same program and year
  • Staff review from the top, against the slots available
  • Outdated results read "Re-screen needed"
  • Draft, rejected and withdrawn are not ranked

A rank never changes an application's official stage.
